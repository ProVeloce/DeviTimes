Using the app

These Terms of Service apply when you access or use the Devi Times billing and point-of-sale application. By signing in or using the app, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

If you use the app for a shop, you confirm that the shop administrator or the person responsible for the shop has authorized your access.

Accounts and access

The app provides Admin and Cashier roles. Shop administrators are responsible for creating, updating, and removing staff accounts and assigning appropriate access. Keep your sign-in details private and promptly tell the shop administrator if you believe an account has been used without permission.

You are responsible for activity performed through your signed-in account. Do not bypass access controls or use another person's credentials.

Shop records and responsibilities

The shop is responsible for the information it enters, including product, inventory, invoice, payment, supplier, and customer records. The shop must ensure that it has the rights and permissions needed to use that information and must provide any notices required for its customers or staff.

Check records for accuracy and make sure staff follow the shop's procedures. The shop remains responsible for its tax, invoicing, recordkeeping, and other legal obligations.

Offline use and synchronization

The app supports offline access to selected data and queues supported changes on the device. Queued changes sync to the configured database when connectivity returns and the same user is signed in. Until a change is confirmed as synced, it may not be available to other devices or users.

Check the app's connection and pending-sync status. Do not clear browser or app storage while changes are pending unless you have another copy of that work. Signing out prevents that user's queued changes from syncing until they sign in again.

Billing and payment records

Devi Times helps shops create invoices and record payment methods, amounts, and references. It does not itself authorize or process card or UPI transactions. Use the relevant payment provider for transaction processing and keep payment credentials out of free-text fields.

The shop is responsible for checking invoice details, taxes, and reports before relying on them for business or statutory purposes.

Google Drive backups

Google Drive backups are optional and available to shop administrators. A backup contains the complete public application database schema and its records, not just the currently visible screen. Confirm that pending offline changes have synced before saving a backup.

Backups are saved to the Google account that the administrator connects. Disconnecting the account does not delete files already created in Drive. The administrator is responsible for protecting that Google account and managing or deleting its backup files.

Third-party services

The app uses a configured Supabase PostgreSQL database for shop records. Google services are used when Google sign-in or Google Drive backup is enabled. Those services may have their own terms and privacy practices, which apply to your use of them.

Availability and changes

App features depend on the device, network connection, configured backend, and any connected third-party service. The app may be unavailable or may not sync while one of those services is interrupted. The shop should verify important records and maintain backups appropriate to its needs.

Features and these terms may change as the app evolves. Updated terms will be published on this page. The current version is the version shown here.

Ending or changing access

A shop administrator can change or revoke a staff account's access. Removing access does not by itself delete records already stored in the shop database or backups already saved to Google Drive. The shop administrator manages those records and copies.
