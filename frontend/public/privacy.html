About this notice

Devi Times is an offline-first billing and point-of-sale application for shop operations. This Privacy Policy describes information handled by the app and its configured backend. A shop administrator manages the shop's users, settings, and business records.

Information in the app

The app processes information that a shop or its staff enters or generates while using it. Depending on the shop's use, this can include:

  • Staff account details such as name, login email or username, and role.
  • Products, variants, categories, suppliers, prices, and inventory history.
  • Customer details entered by the shop, such as name, phone, email, address, or tax identifier.
  • Invoices, invoice items, payment method and amount, payment references, and notes.
  • Shop settings and records needed to operate billing, inventory, and reporting features.

The app records payment information entered by the shop. It does not itself authorize or process card or UPI transactions.

Offline storage and synchronization

To support offline workflows, the app stores selected previously loaded data and pending changes in browser storage on the device. Supported changes are queued and sent to the configured database when a connection is available and the same user is signed in. The app shows connection and pending-sync status; a queued change is not yet confirmed as saved in the database.

Signing out removes the app's login token but does not automatically erase all cached records or queued changes from the device. Queued changes will not sync until the relevant user signs in again. Clearing browser or app storage can remove unsynced work, so check the sync status before clearing it.

Browser storage is tied to the device and browser profile. Secure shared devices, restrict access to the browser profile, and use the device's storage controls when removing local data.

Accounts and access

The app uses staff accounts and Admin or Cashier roles to control access to shop features. The signed-in profile and session token are stored in the browser so the app can maintain the session. Administrators manage shop accounts and should grant access only to people who need it.

If Google sign-in is enabled, Google provides account information needed to verify and sign in to the app. Using Google sign-in does not give the app access to a person's Google Drive unless that person separately connects Drive for backups.

Google services and backups

Google Drive backup is optional. When an administrator connects a Google account, the app receives and stores the account email and a refresh token that the server encrypts. The app uses Google's Drive file scope to create and manage backup files created by Devi Times.

A Drive backup contains a complete dump of the application's public database schema and its records. It can therefore include the information listed in this policy, login password hashes, connected Drive account emails, and encrypted Drive refresh tokens. It does not include Supabase-managed schemas or the server's token-encryption key.

Disconnecting a Google account revokes the saved connection but does not delete backups already created in that account. Review and remove those files directly in Google Drive. Google services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

How information is used and shared

The app uses account and shop information to authenticate staff, provide billing and inventory features, keep supported offline work in sync, and create backups when an administrator requests them. The configured database is hosted using Supabase PostgreSQL. Google processes information when Google sign-in or Drive backup is used.

Shop administrators decide what customer information to enter and are responsible for providing any notices or obtaining any permissions required for their use of that information. The app does not act as a payment processor.

Retention and deletion

The app does not publish a universal retention period for shop records. The shop administrator manages records and staff access in the app and should keep records only as long as needed for the shop's requirements.

Cached data and pending changes may remain in browser storage after sign-out. Backups already created in Google Drive remain there until deleted from that account. Disconnecting Drive does not remove existing backup files.

Security and your choices

Access is controlled through shop accounts and roles. The server encrypts saved Google Drive refresh tokens. No statement in this policy means that every local browser record or every database backup is encrypted by the app on the device or at rest.

Use a secure device, protect your login credentials, and sign out on shared devices. For a request about a staff account or a customer record, contact the shop administrator. An administrator can manage account access and should coordinate requests involving the shop's records.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

For questions or privacy requests, contact your Devi Times shop administrator through the usual shop support channel. A general public support email or mailing address is not listed here.
